# Disturbance contracts for a chain of controlled vehicles

## Context

Future example requested for later consideration by the owner while starting
the active suspension demonstration. A chain of controlled vehicles would test
reuse of local feedback contracts as the number of components grows.

## Outcome

- [ ] Fix an exact vehicle/controller model, disturbance norm and horizon,
      initial spacing/speed region and precise safety or performance property.
- [ ] Prove each vehicle's local feedback contract and a checked interconnection
      theorem, retaining correlations introduced by shared disturbances.
- [ ] Prove a concrete multi-vehicle bound and state its dependence on chain length.
- [ ] Claim string stability or a length-independent bound only with an explicit
      quantified theorem and checked gain conditions, not individual stability.
- [ ] Include hostile controller/gain cases and reusable Lean certificates.
